📐 Biomechanical Metric Formulations

1. Lead Elbow Angle

Ideal: 100°–155°. Evaluates top-hand dominance and elbow elevation through swing contact line. Lower = dropped elbow, poor control.

2. Spine Posture Lean

Ideal: 85°–95°. Angle of the spine relative to the vertical plumb gravity line. Greater deviation = falling away or into the shot.

3. Head-Over-Knee Offset

Ideal: < 0.10. Normalised lateral distance of nose from front knee. Higher = head falling away from line of ball.

4. Foot Stride Vector

Straight Drive: 0°–15° | Cover Drive: 35°–55°. Discriminates shot direction from stride angle at contact moment.

5. Wrist Velocity (Contact)

Used by the ContactDetector to fire per-delivery events. Spike above 0.018 normalised units triggers shot classification.

6. Hip-Shoulder Rotation

Lateral twist between hip plane and shoulder plane. Used in the 10-feature shot classification model to distinguish pull from drive shots.

🏆 Personalized AI Coaching Drills

Drill 1: High Elbow Hanging BallRecommended

Fix dropped lead elbow and keep ball along the ground with suspended ball drills.

Target Metric: Maintain Elbow Angle between 120°–145°
Drill 2: Head-Over-Ball Drop RoutineBalance

Drop stationary balls from eye height to reinforce head alignment over front knee.

Target Metric: Head-over-Knee offset < 0.05
Drill 3: Spine Alignment Wall TouchPosture

Practice strokes with back against a wall guide to train upright spine lean and prevent falling away.

Target Metric: Spine Lean within 85°–95°
Drill 4: Cover Drive Corridor ConeFootwork

Place a cone at extra cover. Drive through the cone corridor to train correct stride angle and timing.

Target Metric: Foot Stride Angle 35°–55° (Cover Drive)
